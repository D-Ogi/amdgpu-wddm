- **The instrument disturbed the measurement, and had done so since E03.** The noise floor for E05 (two sweeps with nothing changed in between) showed 36 registers differing, among them every `GCVM_INVALIDATE_ENGn_SEM` reading 1 the first time and 0 afterwards: a read acquires the semaphore. `CP_*_HEADER_DUMP` advance per read, and reading `GRBM_GFX_CNTL` latches `GRBM_READ_ERROR`. That explains the "Timeout waiting for sem acquire in VM flush" in the E03 dmesg, which I had recorded in M24 as amdgpu's normal behaviour on this unit: our own pre-driver sweep had taken `MMVM_INVALIDATE_ENG17_SEM`, and E01, which did no such sweep, has no timeout. Facts M24 corrected, M25 added, `docs/init-sequence.md` and the wishlist (L9: a clean trace) rewritten, the 43 offending offsets removed from both sweep generators by offset, `bc250rd` rebuilt with 5355 allowed offsets and redeployed. Lesson: "was read without a hang" is not the same as "is safe to read"; a noise-floor pair of sweeps belongs at the start of every comparison, not only when something looks odd.
