Association never starts: `wpa_supplicant` sits in `usb_start_wait_urb` (sampled 5 of 5 times), i.e. every register access is a control transfer through the emulated xHCI and the AP drops the idle station first. Side finding: rtw88 switches the dongle from USB 2 to USB 3 mode (`bcdDevice` 0210 -> 0300); Windows treats that as new hardware and forgets the forced WinUSB binding, so the binding has to be repeated once in USB 3 mode.
