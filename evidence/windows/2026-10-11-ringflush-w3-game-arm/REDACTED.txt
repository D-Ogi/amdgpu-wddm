Redaction record for this directory.

No file was edited. Every file is a copy of the raw file, except presents-559.csv and
presents-561.csv, which are extracted from the ETW dumps (see the README).

What was left out of the directory, and why:

- The ETW dumps gpu-B-dump.txt of trials 559 and 561 (364769789 and 304230640 bytes). Their
  SHA-256 values are in the README. The present times that the analysis reads from them are in
  sessions/presents-N.csv.
- The console output of the session runner (run-559.out, run-560.out, run-561.out). It lists the
  deferred log files of the development PC with that PC's user name. The lines that matter are in
  the trial logs, the ETW notes and the thermal guard output, which are here.
- Every shot of the game's main menu. The menu shows the names of the game accounts. The four
  shots here show the game world and its HUD only.
- The plug samples taken during the refused trial 560, because no session ran.
- The lab lock records and the promotion logs of the D3D12 files before the sessions. They hold no
  measurement.

A scan of every text file for IPv4 addresses, the lab host name, MAC addresses, SSIDs, the game
account names and the development PC's user paths found none. The lab user's profile path does not
occur in these files.

What these files do hold, and why it stays:

- Paths of the workspace (P:\BC-250\...) and of the lab's install root (C:\Program Files\amdgpu-wddm,
  C:\BC250\...). They name the files that the harness used. They are not identifiers of a person or
  of a device.
- Truncated SHA-256 values of the driver files. These are build identifiers.
- The board's fan controller identity line in the state files (EC version and build date, customer
  id 0x162B). This identifies the EC firmware. It is not a serial number.
